    What does ✓ DES mean?

    ✓ DES means the stock is in the Daftar Efek Syariah OJK has in force; not in DES means it is not. OJK screens by the criteria of POJK 35/2017, among them interest-bearing debt of at most 45 % of assets and non-halal income of at most 10 % of revenue. Sahm does not recompute or change that status.

    How do I screen sharia stocks?

    In the terminal, screen syariah=ya keeps only sharia stocks and combines with other ratios, such as screen syariah=ya,pe<15,div>3. syariah BBCA,BRIS checks several codes at once.

    Where does the data come from?

    The Daftar Efek Syariah from OJK; prices, ratios and statements from Yahoo Finance; other data from the public sources each result names. Prices are delayed as their sources publish them. Every figure names its source and time; when a source gives no value, Sahm prints n/a.

    Is this a fatwa or investment advice?

    Neither. Sahm is a research aid. For questions of fiqh, turn to the Dewan Syariah Nasional MUI and scholars; investment decisions stay yours.
